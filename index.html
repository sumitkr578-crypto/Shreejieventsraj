FAQ

Common Booking Questions

How early should we book?

For wedding season, booking 2–4 months in advance is recommended. Smaller functions may be possible at shorter notice, subject to availability.

Do you provide services outside Surajgarh?

Yes. Nearby locations in Jhunjhunu district and surrounding areas can be covered depending on the event requirements.

Can we select only one service?

Yes. You may book tent, decoration, catering, DJ, lighting or couple entry separately, or choose a complete package.

How is the final price decided?

Pricing depends on date, venue, guest count, decoration theme, menu and selected equipment or effects.
